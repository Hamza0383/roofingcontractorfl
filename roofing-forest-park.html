Plan for the season

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Forest Park

Forest Park's climate influences roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding these seasonal considerations can help homeowners plan for maintenance and repairs.

☼

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, increasing the risk of leaks and water damage. Homeowners should inspect their roofs for any signs of wear.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection in early spring to catch any potential issues before the rainy season.
☼

Summer

Summer heat can cause shingles to expand and contract, leading to potential damage. Increased humidity may also lead to moss growth.

Tip: Consider a roof cleaning service to remove debris and prevent moss from taking hold.
☼

Fall

Fall is a great time for roofing maintenance as temperatures cool down. Leaves can clog gutters, leading to water overflow.

Tip: Clean gutters and schedule a roof inspection to prepare for winter.
☼

Winter

While winters are mild, occasional cold snaps can lead to condensation issues in attics if roofs aren't properly ventilated.

Tip: Ensure your roof has adequate ventilation to prevent moisture buildup and ice dam formation.

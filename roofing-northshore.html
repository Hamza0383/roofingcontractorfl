Plan for the season

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Northshore

Northshore's climate influences roofing demands throughout the year. Understanding seasonal considerations can help homeowners prepare accordingly.

☼

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains, which can reveal leaks and other issues. It's essential to conduct inspections during this time.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to identify and address any potential leaks early.
☼

Summer

Summer heat can lead to shingle curling and other heat-related issues. Regular maintenance is crucial.

Tip: Consider roof ventilation solutions to combat heat buildup and prolong your roof's lifespan.
☼

Fall

Fall brings falling leaves that can clog gutters and lead to water damage. Regular cleaning is necessary.

Tip: Schedule gutter cleaning to prevent clogs and ensure proper drainage before winter.
☼

Winter

Winter storms can cause snow accumulation, leading to potential leaks and ice dams.

Tip: Ensure your roof is clear of debris to prevent ice dams and schedule any necessary repairs beforehand.

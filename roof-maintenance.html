The service timeline

How Does Roof Maintenance Work?

01

Initial Inspection

We start with a thorough inspection of your roof, looking for signs of wear, damage, or potential issues. This typically takes about one hour, depending on the roof size.

02

Cleaning Debris

Next, we remove debris, leaves, and moss from your roof and gutters. This step prevents moisture retention and promotes proper drainage, taking about 30 minutes.

03

Repairing Damages

Our team addresses any identified issues, such as replacing missing shingles or repairing flashing. This step varies in time based on the extent of the damage, typically taking 1-3 hours.

04

Ventilation Check

We inspect your roof's ventilation system to ensure it functions correctly. Proper ventilation is crucial to preventing moisture buildup and prolonging your roof's life, taking about 30 minutes.

05

Final Report

After completing the maintenance, we provide a detailed report of our findings and any recommendations for future care. This ensures you stay informed about your roof's condition.

Problems we solve

Common Roofing Problems We Solve

Our roof maintenance services address several common issues that can lead to significant damage if ignored.

01

Roof leaks due to damaged shingles

Ignoring leaks can lead to water damage inside your home, resulting in costly repairs. We identify and repair leaks during our maintenance checks, ensuring your roof remains watertight.

02

Moss growth on roofs

Moss can trap moisture, leading to shingle deterioration and potential leaks. Our maintenance services include moss removal and preventive treatments to keep your roof clean and dry.

03

Poor ventilation causing heat buildup

Inadequate ventilation can lead to increased energy costs and premature roof aging. We assess and improve your roof's ventilation system, enhancing energy efficiency and longevity.

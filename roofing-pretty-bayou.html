How Seasons Affect Roofing in Pretty Bayou

Pretty Bayou's climate influences roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal considerations can help homeowners maintain their roofs effectively.

☼

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains that can expose roofing issues, making inspections essential.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to identify and address any damage before the summer storms.
☼

Summer

High temperatures can cause shingles to deteriorate faster, leading to potential leaks.

Tip: Consider roof maintenance to ensure your roof can withstand the summer heat.
☼

Fall

Fall leaves can clog gutters, leading to water damage if not cleared regularly.

Tip: Schedule gutter cleaning to prevent water buildup and protect your roof.
☼

Winter

Winter weather can lead to ice dams, causing leaks and structural issues.

Tip: Ensure your roof is properly insulated and ventilated to prevent ice dam formation.

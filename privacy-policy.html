Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Effective date: 2023-09-13

At Roofing Contractor Florida, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and how we use, share, and protect that information.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any other details you share when you contact us or request a service.

We may also collect information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, and device information.

How We Use Information

We use your information to provide our services, respond to your inquiries, and improve our customer service.

We may also use your information to send you marketing communications, if you have opted in to receive them.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your device.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Analytics and Third-Party Tools

We use third-party analytics tools to collect and analyze information about our website traffic and usage.

These tools may collect information such as your IP address and pages visited, but they do not identify individual users.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website or conducting our business.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

When your information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

User Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years old.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any updates.

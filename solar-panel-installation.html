How Does Solar Panel Installation Work?

01

Consultation

The process begins with a consultation to assess your energy needs and roof suitability. This step typically lasts about an hour.

Our team designs a customized solar system based on your energy consumption and roof layout. This phase can take up to a week.

Installation

Installation generally takes one to three days, depending on system size. Our certified team ensures compliance with Florida Building Code.

Recognizing the signs that you need roofing services in Bayou can save you from more extensive damage. Here are some common indicators that it's time to call our Bayou roof contractor.

2. Missing or damaged shingles

3. Moss or algae growth

4. Increased energy bills

If you notice a spike in your energy bills, it may be due to poor insulation or ventilation in your roof, indicating a need for inspection.

5. Visible sagging in the roof
